MONSTRUM

A single monstrous face, always new.

The machine invents a monster and never stops changing it: a face made of geometric shapes in the three primary colours, white and black. Eyes, horns, teeth, mouths, beaks, ears and tentacles are reinvented at every moment — how many, where, of what shape and colour — while the face breathes, blinks, looks around, chews and sways, at ever-changing speeds and rhythms.

monstrum: from monere, to warn. Cicero heard in it monstrare, to show.

A project by Jacopo Natoli, 2026
